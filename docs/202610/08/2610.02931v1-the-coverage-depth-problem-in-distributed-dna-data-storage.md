## 摘要
DNA测序中的随机采样会产生重复读取，从而增加检索延迟和测序成本。我们研究在无噪声均匀采样下，分布式DNA存储中用于完整消息恢复的覆盖深度问题，其中链被划分到 $M$ 个容器中，每轮从每个容器独立地有放回地采样一条链。对于任意线性码和有序划分，我们推导出恢复时间分布与期望的精确公式。我们证明，只要MDS码存在，它们对每个固定划分都是最优的，并建立期望总读取成本的通用下界及其取等条件。对于MDS码，我们识别出能够真正节省总读取次数的容器大小区间，以及仅提供并行性而不改变渐近测序成本的区间。对于单纯形码，我们证明 $q$ 元单纯形码在同参数码中，在同构意义下是唯一的单容器最小化码，从而解决了 Bertuzzo、Ravagnani 和 Yaakobi 最近提出的一个猜想。我们进一步构造出达到最小总读取成本的划分，并推导出中间划分和平衡划分的界。这些结果阐明了分布式采样何时仅降低延迟，何时也降低测序成本。

## Abstract
Random sampling in DNA sequencing produces repeated reads, increasing retrieval latency and sequencing cost. We study the coverage-depth problem for full-message recovery in distributed DNA storage under noiseless uniform sampling, where strands are partitioned among $M$ containers and one strand is independently sampled with replacement from each container per round. For arbitrary linear codes and ordered partitions, we derive exact formulas for the recovery-time distribution and expectation. We prove that MDS codes, whenever they exist, are optimal for every fixed partition, and establish a universal lower bound on the expected total read cost together with its equality conditions. For MDS codes, we identify container-size regimes that yield genuine savings in total reads and regimes that provide only parallelism without changing the asymptotic sequencing cost. For simplex codes, we prove that the $q$-ary simplex code is, up to isomorphism, the unique single-container minimizer among codes with the same parameters, resolving a recent conjecture by Bertuzzo, Ravagnani, and Yaakobi. We further construct a partition attaining the minimum total read cost and derive bounds for intermediate and balanced partitions. These results clarify when distributed sampling reduces latency alone and when it also reduces sequencing cost.
